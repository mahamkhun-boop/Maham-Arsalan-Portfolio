MY APPROACH

Strategy before sentences.

The writing changes with the intent. The thinking behind it stays consistent.

01

Search intent

I start with what the reader is trying to accomplish, then choose the angle and structure that best answers that need.

02

Structure

Listicles, FAQs, guides and narrative essays all have their place. Format follows the topic rather than habit.

03

Link placement

When link building is part of the brief, links belong inside sentences that make sense without them — never bolted on.

04

Authority

For health and other high-trust subjects, credible organizations, named studies and responsible framing matter.

05

Local targeting

Geo-modified angles and local references can turn a generic article into content that serves a specific market.

06

Content clusters

One broad topic can become several purposeful entry points when different audiences and search intents deserve different pages.
